Institutional Activities & Student Chapters at ARMIET

Alamuri Ratnamala Institute of Engineering & Technology provides structured institutional platforms supporting student engineering innovation and civic engagement.

MESA

Mechanical Engineering Students Association coordinating Technical Quizzes, Design Contests, Paper Presentations, RoboRace, and RoboWar prototyping.

NSS Unit

National Service Scheme unit at ARMIET focusing on community service, blood donation camps, cleanliness drives, and social welfare awareness.

IEEE & ISTE Chapters

Student branches facilitating national technical symposiums, research paper workshops, and industry expert interactions.

Sports & Athletics

Campus sports facilities and inter-departmental athletic tournaments (Spardha) promoting fitness, team spirit, and collegiate sportsmanship.
