About ARMIET Club & Event Portal

An institutional student activity management portal supporting co-curricular growth, technical learning, and community engagement.

Alamuri Ratnamala Institute of Engineering & Technology (ARMIET)

Alamuri Ratnamala Institute of Engineering & Technology (ARMIET) is located in Shahapur, Thane, Maharashtra. The institute provides an academic environment focused on engineering education, technical skills, and holistic student development across undergraduate and postgraduate disciplines.

Beyond classroom instruction, ARMIET actively encourages students to take part in technical competitions, cultural festivals, athletic tournaments, and community welfare programs through recognized student bodies and chapters.

Student Organizations & Activities

The institute hosts active student associations and units that provide opportunities for hands-on learning, teamwork, and social responsibility:

  • MESA: Mechanical Engineering Students Association coordinating Technical Quizzes, Design Contests, RoboRace, RoboWar, and Paper Presentations.
  • NSS Unit: National Service Scheme organizing blood donation camps, cleanliness drives, and community awareness activities.
  • IEEE & ISTE: Professional student branches conducting seminars, workshops, and technical symposiums.
  • Sports & Athletics: Inter-departmental sporting events (Spardha) and physical fitness activities across campus facilities.

Technical Architecture

The Student Club & Event Management Portal is built with an enterprise pure Java web architecture:

  • Backend Core: Pure Java (JDK 17) with Jakarta Servlet API 6.0
  • Web Container: Apache Tomcat 10.1
  • Database: MySQL 8.0+ / 8.4 with relational foreign keys and unique constraints
  • Data Access: JDBC PreparedStatement implementations protecting against SQL injection
  • Frontend UI: Semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3 Grid & Flexbox, and vanilla JavaScript without heavy SPA bloat
